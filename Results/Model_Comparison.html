How PCA and k are chosen

PCA: test fixed counts 5, 50 and 200, plus targets retaining 90%, 95% and 99% of variance. A variance target selects a component count separately on each fold’s training rows. PCA centres inputs; no standard scaling or whitening is used.

k: test odd values 1–25. Choose the highest mean grouped-CV accuracy, then macro-F1. Exact ties prefer smaller k, then candidate order. The error curve can reveal a plateau or elbow, but classification does not always have a unique elbow. Variance retention alone does not guarantee the best floor classification.

Scope and limitations

  • Floor prediction, not room or metre-level positioning.
  • Missing signal 100 → −110 is an explicit assumption; genuine zeros remain unchanged.
  • The paper’s k=1 and PCA=5/50/200 settings are included, but its full sampling/preprocessing/repeated-CV protocol is not reproduced.
  • A combined model might behave differently if BUILDINGID were added as a feature; that is a separate experiment.
  • Do not chase scores on this already inspected benchmark and call it an untouched final test.
  • No-signal abstention is not implemented.
