6. Interpretation and reproducibility

Repeated locations and duplicate fingerprints may cross random CV folds. High CV accuracy is not evidence of unseen-location performance. Building-wise models require a known building ID; no-signal scans receive a prediction without abstention. PCA and forest settings are fixed design choices.

Evaluate loads saved pipelines only: no retraining, PCA refitting or validation-based k selection. All 520 WAP signals are used, with 100 mapped to −110 and genuine zeros retained.
